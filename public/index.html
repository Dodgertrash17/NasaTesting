Welcome to SkyBlink, the astronomy blinker

Since 2025, NASA's SPHEREx telescope has photographed the entire sky every six months in 102 colors of infrared light. Hidden in those pictures are things that move: asteroids, comets, nearby stars, and cold brown dwarfs drifting through our corner of the galaxy.

SkyBlink lines up images of the same patch of sky taken months apart and flips between them, the same trick that found Pluto in 1930. Anything that jumps or flickers is worth a closer look. No telescope or astronomy degree needed.
